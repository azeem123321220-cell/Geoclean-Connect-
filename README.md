🌿 GeoClean Connect

Report it. Track it. Get it cleaned.

GeoClean Connect is a smart city civic reporting and complaint management platform that helps citizens report public issues and track them until they are resolved.

🌐 Live Website

https://gocleangogreen.lovable.app/

🎯 Problem Statement

- Delayed responses to civic complaints.
- No centralized complaint management system.
- Difficulty identifying the exact location of issues.
- Lack of transparency in complaint status.
- Manual complaint handling and poor communication.

💡 Our Solution

GeoClean Connect provides a simple and efficient way to report and manage civic issues.

- 📸 Photo Reporting: Capture and upload photos of civic problems.
- 📍 GPS Location: Pinpoint the exact location of an issue.
- 🔄 Smart Routing: Forward complaints to the appropriate department.
- 📊 Live Tracking: Track complaint status until resolution.
- 🏢 Official Dashboard: Manage complaints through a centralized dashboard.

🛠️ Tech Stack

- Frontend: React 19, Tailwind CSS v4
- Backend: Supabase
- Database: PostgreSQL
- Maps: OpenStreetMap and Leaflet
- Development Tool: Lovable AI

👥 Who Can Benefit?

- Municipal corporations and local authorities
- Village panchayats and municipalities
- Smart city agencies
- Sanitation and public works departments
- NGOs, volunteers, and citizens

🚀 Future Enhancements

- AI-based issue identification and prioritization
- Mobile application with SMS and push notifications
- Telugu, Hindi, and English language support
- Hotspot analytics for recurring civic problems
- Smart bin and IoT sensor integration

🌍 Our Vision

To build cleaner, smarter, and more transparent cities by connecting citizens with the authorities responsible for resolving civic issues.
